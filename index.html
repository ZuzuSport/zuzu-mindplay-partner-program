FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who qualifies to become a Zuzu MindPlay partner?
We welcome applications from pediatric therapists, occupational therapists, physical therapists, kids' movement coaches, youth sports coaches, child-development professionals, pediatric clinics, parent creators, school and enrichment coordinators, and community leaders who work with kids and families.
Is there a cost to apply?
No. Applying to the Zuzu MindPlay Partner Program is completely free. There are no fees to become a partner.
How does the revenue share work?
When a family you refer becomes a paid subscriber after their trial, you earn revenue share based on your partner tier: Standard (10%), Professional (15%), or Strategic (20%) of confirmed subscription revenue for the first 3 paid months. For annual subscriptions, commission applies to the first annual payment only unless otherwise agreed.
Can I use Zuzu MindPlay as a clinical or therapeutic tool?
Zuzu MindPlay is a family-friendly brain-body movement app — it is not a medical treatment, therapy tool, or diagnostic resource. Partners are asked to describe it as a home activity that supports active screen time, movement, focus, and confidence.
How long does the approval process take?
We review all applications personally. Most applicants hear back within 5–7 business days. You'll only be contacted if your application is approved.
What support do partners receive?
All approved partners receive a unique partner code, access to a partner portal, onboarding materials, and marketing assets. Professional and Strategic partners receive additional support and co-branded resources.
What age group is Zuzu MindPlay designed for?
Zuzu MindPlay is designed for children and families. The app features short brain-body missions suitable for a range of childhood ages. Specific age guidance is provided in the app and onboarding materials.
